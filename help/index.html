Help & FAQ

Quick answers about Davey. Still stuck? Email support@daveypersonalfinance.com or use Settings › Contact Support in the app, which adds your app version and a diagnostic ID so we can help faster.

Connecting banks

How do I connect my bank?

On iPhone or Mac, open Settings › Accounts, tap +, then Connect a Bank. On the web, use Connect an account in the sidebar, or under More on a phone. That one step covers checking, savings, credit cards, loans, mortgages, and investments. Davey uses Plaid: you sign in inside Plaid's secure screen (on Mac, in your web browser). Davey never sees your password, and access is read-only: Davey can't move money.

My bank says "sign in again" or shows Reconnect

Banks ask you to sign in again from time to time. Tap Reconnect next to the bank in Accounts and sign in; your history stays.

On Mac, nothing happened when I tapped Connect a Bank

Davey opens Plaid in your default web browser. Finish there; when your browser asks to open Davey, allow it. If you closed the page, use Open Again in Accounts.

Can I track accounts without connecting a bank?

Yes. On iPhone or Mac, open Settings › Accounts, tap +, then Add Manual Account. On the web, open Connect an account and choose Add a manual account instead. Use it for cash, property, a car, gold, crypto, loans or anything else, and update the balance whenever the value changes. Davey keeps the history.

Views, budgets and business

What is a View?

A View is a group of accounts, like Personal or Business. Switch Views and the whole app follows. Star a View to open Davey to it every time.

How does Personal vs Business work?

Mark an account as Business when you add or edit it. Davey creates a Business View with profit & loss and an estimated tax set-aside. These are estimates, not tax advice.

Davey AI

What can I ask?

Questions about your own money in plain English, like "How much did I spend on dining last month?" Answers can be wrong and aren't financial, tax or legal advice.

Privacy and security

Where is my data?

On your devices and in your own iCloud. Bank access keys are stored encrypted on Davey's server, never on your phone. We never sell your data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete Account. Davey disconnects every bank, deletes your data on our server, and erases the data on your devices and in iCloud.
