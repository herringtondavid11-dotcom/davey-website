Features

Everything you need to run your money

One calm, clear place for your accounts, spending, budgets and goals.

Connect everything

Every account, one clear picture

Link checking, savings, credit cards, loans and investments securely through Plaid — or track accounts by hand. Name them your way, and group them into Views like Personal, Business or Rentals.

  • Thousands of US banks, card issuers and lenders
  • Nicknames, Personal or Business, and hide-from-budget for every account
  • Separate Views so personal and business money never blur
  • If a bank won't connect, Davey explains why and what to try — or import a CSV from your brokerage
See where it goes

Spending, finally make sense of it

Every transaction is categorized automatically. See your top categories and merchants, and how this month compares with the last.

  • Smart categories and rules you control
  • Top merchants and a spending calendar
  • Search, split and recategorize in seconds
Budgets that work

Stay on pace, all month long

Set monthly budgets by category, roll unspent money forward, and get a heads-up before you go over.

  • Alerts at 80% and 100% of a budget
  • Optional rollover for each category
  • A daily allowance that adjusts as you spend
Net worth

Watch your net worth grow

Assets and debts side by side, with the change since last month and a trend you can explore from one month to all time.

  • Assets vs. debts at a glance
  • History from 1 month to all time
  • Cash flow: money in, money out, what's left
Goals

Hit your goals

Give any account a target and Davey shows your progress and when you'll get there — with a nudge at every milestone.

  • Savings goals with projected finish dates
  • Milestone notifications as you grow
  • Investment growth by month, quarter and year
Bills & subscriptions

Never miss a charge

Davey spots recurring bills and subscriptions, flags price increases, and reminds you the day before a bill is due.

  • Upcoming bills for the next two weeks
  • Subscription detection with price-change flags
  • Alerts for large charges and low balances
Credit cards

Know what you owe, and what's left

Every card in one place: what you owe, your limit, available credit and utilization. Card payments count as transfers, so spending is never counted twice.

  • Utilization bars that turn gold and terracotta as you climb
  • Statement balance, due date and APR
  • A reminder three days before each payment is due
Mortgages & loans

See the finish line on every loan

Mortgages, home equity lines, auto, student and personal loans, linked or added by hand. Davey shows your payoff date, interest, home equity and what extra payments would save.

  • Full amortization schedule and a payoff simulator
  • Refinance check: new payment, break-even and lifetime savings
  • A Debt overview with an avalanche or snowball payoff plan
Davey AI

Ask anything about your money

"How much did I spend on dining last month?" "When will I pay off my mortgage?" Davey AI answers from your own accounts, with the numbers behind every answer.

  • Scoped to the screen and View you're on
  • Budgets, trends, subscriptions, cash flow, goals and loans
  • Never shown more than the last four digits of an account
Business & tax

Run the numbers for your business

Business Views get profit and loss, Schedule C and E line items, quarterly estimated taxes and a deduction finder. Rental mortgages feed mortgage interest automatically.

  • Quarterly estimates with reminders before each due date
  • Mileage, assets and depreciation, contractors and 1099s
  • A CPA-ready export when it's time to file

Share with your partner

Household Sharing gives one partner the same accounts, budgets and goals, each with their own sign-in. Premium covers both of you.

On your Mac, too

A full Mac app with a sidebar, sortable transaction tables, keyboard shortcuts and CSV drag-and-drop, synced through iCloud.

Help when a bank won't connect

Plain-English reasons, tips for your bank, one-tap retry and a ready-to-send report for Plaid support.

    Security & privacy

    Your money stays yours

    Davey is built to see as little as possible and to keep what it sees safe.

    Read-only access

    Connections through Plaid are read-only. Davey can't move money.

    256-bit encryption

    Bank access keys are stored encrypted with AES-256 on our server, never on your phone.

    No passwords stored

    You sign in to your bank through Plaid. Davey never sees or keeps your bank credentials.

    Face ID app lock

    Davey locks with Face ID by default, and you can hide balances with a tap.

    We never sell your data

    No ads, no data brokers. Your budgets sync through your own iCloud account.

    Delete anytime

    Delete your account and data from Settings whenever you like.

    Read the details in our Privacy Policy.
