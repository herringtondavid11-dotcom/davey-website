Cancel & Refunds

Davey Premium is billed by Apple through your Apple Account, so Apple handles cancellations and refunds.

Cancel your subscription

iPhone: open Davey › Settings › Manage Subscription, or the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Davey › Cancel Subscription.

Mac: open the App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions › Manage › Davey › Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. Premium stays on until the period you paid for ends; then Davey switches to the free plan and keeps all your data.

Refunds

Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose Davey Premium, and select Request a refund. Apple decides refunds under its policies. If a refund is approved, Premium ends for that purchase.

Need help?

Email support@daveypersonalfinance.com. We can't see or change your Apple billing, but we're happy to help.
